[credits] SERVICE CREDITS. A credit entitles the Client to one unit of a named W5D service under the published terms of that service. Credits are personal to the Client: they cannot be transferred, are not redeemable for money and are not a financial instrument.

[no-return] NO RETURN. Credits earn nothing by themselves. They are prepayment for services, not an investment.

[delivery] DELIVERY. Delivery follows the published quality rules (sampling, corrections, evidence). Nothing in this contract promises an outcome, a result or an income to the Client.

[law] LAW AND DISPUTES. Governing law, competent court, invoicing and the tax treatment of the escrowed sum are completed after the advice of counsel and the accountant. Until then the template must not be signed.
